Hand-off Guidelines

Follow these steps every time you return or collect an item. They protect your privacy and stop fraudulent claims.

Golden rule: hand items over only at a campus hand-off hub. Never in a hostel room, a car or a quiet corner.

1. Before you meet
  • Use the in-app anonymous chat only. Do not share your phone number, email, room number or social media handles.
  • Agree on one hub and a time inside its opening hours. Check that its badge says Open.
  • Tell a friend or roommate where and when you are going.
2. Choose the right hub
  • For phones, laptops, wallets, ID cards or anything valuable, pick a 24/7 security post.
  • After dark, always use a 24/7 security post.
  • Hostel desks, the Library and the SCA Office are fine for everyday items during their opening hours.
3. Prove ownership
  • The owner must answer the secret verification question set when the item was reported.
  • Finders: do not reveal details of the item in advance. Let the owner describe it first.
  • Bring your student ID to the hub so the desk can confirm who you are.
4. At the hub
  • Hand the item over in front of the desk or security staff.
  • Check the item together before you leave.
  • Never pay or accept payment for returning an item.
5. After the hand-off
  • Mark the item as Resolved / Returned on your dashboard.
  • Close the chat so the conversation does not stay open.
  • Report anything that felt wrong to campus security.

Red flags - stop and walk away: the person cannot answer the verification question, asks you to meet somewhere else, asks for your phone number, or rushes you. Take the item to the nearest security post instead.

Which hub should I use?

Not sure what to do? Go to the nearest 24/7 security post and ask for help.
