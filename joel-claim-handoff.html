How to Claim & Hand Off

Four steps from finding your item to closing the case safely.

0 of 4 steps done

  1. Search the catalog by category, location and date, then open the item page and read its description and photo.

  2. Press Claim and answer the ownership question shown on the item page. Only the real owner will know the answer.

  3. Use the anonymous chat to agree on a hub and a time inside its opening hours. Never share your phone number or email.

  4. Hand the item over in front of the hub staff, then mark it Resolved / Returned on your dashboard.
